Privacy Policy

Your words stay
on your Mac.

QoQ does not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data. Text and images you ask QoQ to process remain on your device.

The short answer

Information we collect

QoQ does not collect personal information, usage data, analytics, or advertising identifiers. The app does not require an account and does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Inside macOS

On-device processing

When you translate selected text, translate a screen area, or extract text from the screen, QoQ processes that content on your Mac using Apple’s macOS Translation and Vision frameworks. QoQ does not send the selected text, captured image, recognized text, or translation to the developer or to a third-party translation service.

◆

Your content is used only to perform the action you request and is not retained by QoQ afterward.

Only when needed

System permissions

QoQ may request macOS permissions needed to read selected text or capture a screen area. These permissions are used only to provide the feature you activate. You can review or revoke them at any time in macOS System Settings.

This website

Website data

This is a static website and does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies set by QoQ. The site is hosted with GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process basic technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own privacy terms.

Keeping this current

Changes to this policy

If QoQ’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised. Material changes will be described clearly before they take effect.
